Privacy rights

Your Privacy Rights

App: Acupressure (Android) · Last updated: 8 November 2026

This page explains the privacy rights you have in relation to the Acupressure app and gives you exact, step-by-step instructions on how to exercise each of them — in the App, in the health platform on your device, or by contacting us.

Good news: the App has no user accounts and keeps your health data on your device. That means you can exercise almost every right yourself, immediately, without waiting for us.

1. Summary of your rights

Right What it means How to exercise it
Right to be informed Know what data is used and why. Read the Privacy Policy and the in-app onboarding screen.
Right of access See the data the App holds about you. Open App → History / Saved points, or view your records in Health Connect / Huawei Health.
Right to data portability Receive your data in a machine-readable format. Use App → Settings → Export my data, or export from Health Connect / Huawei Health.
Right to rectification Correct inaccurate data. Fix the record in the source app (Health Connect / Huawei Health); the App always reads the corrected value.
Right to erasure Delete your data. App → Settings → Delete all data, or uninstall the App. See section 3.
Right to withdraw consent Stop the App from reading health data at any time. Revoke permissions in Health Connect or Huawei Health. See section 2.
Right to restrict or object Limit how your data is processed. Revoke permissions (stops all processing) or e-mail us.
Right to lodge a complaint Complain to a data protection authority. Contact your national supervisory authority. See section 6.

2. Control health data access (consent)

The App can read health data only after you grant permission, and only for the data types you approve (steps, calories, active hours, activity records, heart rate, stress, sleep). You can review or revoke that permission at any time; revoking it takes effect immediately and prevents any further reads.

2.1 Google Health Connect (Android)

  1. Open the Health Connect app (or Settings → Apps → Health Connect on Android 14+).
  2. Go to Data and access → App permissions.
  3. Select Acupressure.
  4. Turn off individual data types, or choose Remove access to revoke everything.

To delete the data that Health Connect stores on your device, use Health Connect → Delete all data (this deletes records for all apps, not only Acupressure).

2.2 HUAWEI Health Service Kit (Huawei devices)

  1. Open the Huawei Health app.
  2. Go to Me → Privacy → Authorization management (naming may vary slightly by version).
  3. Find Acupressure and turn off the authorization, or tap it and choose Revoke / Cancel authorization.

You can also remove the authorization from the App itself: App → Settings → Connected health service → Disconnect.

3. Delete your data

Data Where it lives How to delete it
Saved points, history, settings, language Only on your device, in the App's private storage App → Settings → Delete all data, or uninstall the App
Text you typed describing how you feel Processed to generate the answer; not retained by us Deleted automatically after the response is generated
Anonymous summary sent for a recommendation Transient request to the AI service Not stored after the answer is produced; short-lived technical logs expire within 7 days
Raw health records (steps, calories, active hours, activity records, heart rate, stress, sleep) Health Connect / Huawei Health Manage or delete them in the respective platform (section 2)

Uninstalling the App removes all data the App stored on your device. Because we keep no account and no server-side profile, there is nothing else associated with you to delete.

4. Access and export your data

  • In the App: Settings → Export my data produces a JSON file with your saved recommendations and preferences, which you can share or store yourself.
  • From the health platform: Health Connect and Huawei Health both provide their own export tools and are the authoritative source of your raw health records.
  • From us: e-mail us and we will confirm what we process — in practice, this is limited to the transient request described in the Privacy Policy.

5. Contact us about a privacy request

E-mail: tsaritov@gmail.com
Please include the app name and describe the request (access, deletion, objection, etc.).

We answer within 30 days of receiving your request, and we will tell you if we need more time. We may ask for information needed to protect your data from unauthorised deletion requests — but since we hold no account data, in most cases no verification is possible or necessary.

6. Complaints

If you believe your data has been processed unlawfully, you can complain to the data protection authority in your country of residence, place of work or place of the alleged infringement. In the EU/EEA you can find your authority through the European Data Protection Board. We would appreciate the chance to resolve the issue first — please write to us.

7. Our commitments

  • No sale of personal data, ever.
  • No advertising or tracking SDKs in the App.
  • No reading of health data without your explicit permission.
  • No upload of raw health records — only an anonymous, coarse summary when you request a recommendation.
  • No dark patterns: revoking consent is as easy as granting it.

8. Medical disclaimer

Acupressure provides general wellness information and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Consult a qualified healthcare provider for any health concerns.
