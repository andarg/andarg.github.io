About the app

Feeling tense, stressed, or having trouble sleeping? Acupressure reads your heart rate, HRV, stress level and sleep quality from Health Connect (Google) or Huawei Health Kit, then uses AI to suggest the right acupressure point for you right now.

How it works

  1. Connect your health store — Health Connect on Android, or Huawei Health Kit on Huawei devices.
  2. Describe how you feel in your own words.
  3. Get a personalized acupressure point with clear instructions and a recommended duration.

Features

  • Personalized recommendations based on your heart rate, stress score, HRV, sleep, and your own description of how you feel.
  • Clear guidance for each point, including location and how long to hold it.
  • Multilingual: English, Russian, and Vietnamese.
  • Privacy first: your health data stays on your device. Only an anonymous summary is sent to the AI recommendation service.

Disclaimer. Acupressure provides general wellness information and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Consult a qualified healthcare provider for any health concerns.
