Privacy Policy

App: Acupressure (Android) · Last updated: 3 November 2026 · Effective date: 3 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Acupressure app ("the App", "we", "us") accesses, how it is used, where it is stored and which rights you have. We designed the App so that your health data stays on your device and only an anonymous, aggregated summary ever leaves it.

In short: we do not create user accounts, we do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and your raw health records are never uploaded to us. A short anonymous summary is sent to an AI service only at the moment you ask for a recommendation, in order to generate that recommendation.

1. Who we are

The App is published by the Acupressure developer (the data controller for the limited processing described in this Policy). You can reach us at tsaritov@gmal.com.

For an overview of your rights and step-by-step instructions on how to exercise them, see Your Privacy Rights.

2. Information the App accesses

2.1 Health and fitness data (read-only)

With your explicit permission, the App reads selected health data from the health platform on your device. The App does not write data back to these platforms.

Data type Source Purpose
Heart rate Health Connect (Google) / Huawei Health Kit Assess your current physiological state
Heart rate variability (HRV) Health Connect / Huawei Health Kit Estimate stress and recovery
Stress level Health Connect / Huawei Health Kit Choose an appropriate acupressure point
Sleep sessions and sleep quality Health Connect / Huawei Health Kit Account for rest and fatigue in the recommendation

Access is requested per data type through the platform permission dialog. You may grant or deny each type separately, and you can revoke access at any time (see Your Privacy Rights).

2.2 What you tell us

When you ask for a recommendation, you may describe how you feel in your own words. This text is used only to produce your recommendation.

2.3 App settings

The App stores your language choice, onboarding state, saved recommendations and preferences locally on your device.

2.4 What we do not collect

  • No account, name, e-mail address or phone number.
  • No advertising identifiers (Advertising ID / OAID) and no advertising SDKs.
  • No contacts, call logs, SMS, photos, files, microphone or camera access.
  • No continuous background location tracking.
  • No behavioural profiling or cross-app tracking.

3. How the App uses your information

  • Generate your recommendation. Combine your health metrics and your own description to select an acupressure point, its location and a recommended duration.
  • Keep your history. Show previously suggested points locally so you can revisit them.
  • Operate and secure the service. Process the request, prevent abuse and keep the service reliable.

We do not use your health data for advertising, marketing, profiling, credit scoring, insurance decisions or any purpose incompatible with the ones listed above, and we do not sell or rent it.

4. What leaves your device

Your raw health records and your recommendation history stay on your device. When you request a recommendation, the App sends one request over an encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connection to the AI recommendation service. That request contains:

  • an anonymous summary of your recent metrics — for example a rounded heart-rate value, a stress band (low / moderate / high), an HRV band and an approximate sleep duration;
  • the text you typed describing how you feel;
  • your selected language.

The request does not contain your name, e-mail, phone number, account identifier, advertising identifier, precise location, or the raw health records themselves.

5. Artificial intelligence processing

Recommendations are produced with the help of a third-party large language model service acting as our processor. The anonymous summary described above is transmitted only to generate the answer to your request. We configure the service so that requests are not used to train public models, and we do not authorize its provider to use the content for advertising or profiling. Output is informative only and is not a medical diagnosis.

6. Sharing

We share data only in the limited cases below.

Recipient What is shared Why
AI inference provider (processor) Anonymous summary, your free-text description, language Generate the recommendation
Hosting / infrastructure provider (processor) Transient technical logs without health values Deliver and secure the service
Public authorities Only what is legally required Comply with a valid legal obligation

Google Health Connect and Huawei Health Kit operate under their own privacy policies. We receive data from them only with your permission and never send data back to them.

7. Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

  • Explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR) — for reading health data and for sending the anonymous summary to generate a recommendation. You may withdraw it at any time.
  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) — providing the feature you requested.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — keeping the service secure and preventing abuse.

8. Storage and retention

  • On your device: settings, health summaries and recommendation history are kept until you delete them in the App or uninstall the App.
  • Our servers: we do not build profiles and do not keep a history of your requests. Recommendation requests are processed in memory to produce the answer and are not retained afterwards. Short-lived technical logs that do not contain health values may be kept for up to 7 days for security and debugging, then deleted.

9. Security

  • All network traffic is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS.
  • Only the minimum data needed for a recommendation is transmitted, and health values are reduced to coarse bands and rounded numbers.
  • No accounts and no central database of health records are maintained, which limits the impact of any incident.
  • Local data is stored in the App's private sandbox and is protected by the operating system; other apps cannot read it.

10. International transfers

Our AI and infrastructure processors may process requests on servers located outside your country. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. Because the transmitted summary contains no direct identifiers, the risk associated with such transfers is minimised.

11. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA where applicable). We do not knowingly process data of children. If you are a minor, use the App only with the consent of a parent or legal guardian. If you believe a child has used the App, contact us and we will help you delete the local data.

12. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, export, restrict or delete your data, to withdraw consent and to object to processing, and to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. Because the App has no accounts and stores your data on your device, most of these rights are exercised directly in the App or in the health platform settings. Full instructions are on the Your Privacy Rights page.

13. Medical disclaimer

Acupressure provides general wellness information and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider about medical concerns, and call emergency services in case of an emergency.

14. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy to reflect changes in the App or in the law. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page always shows the current version, and material changes will be announced in the App or in the store listing before they take effect.
